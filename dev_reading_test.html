Kortpris reading test

Development tool, not part of the app. It makes fake phone photos of real cards (tilted, badly lit, blurred, noisy, JPEG-compressed), runs them through the app's own reader and search, and scores the result. The fake photos are the same on every run, so scores can be compared before and after a change.
