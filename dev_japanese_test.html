Kortpris Japanese card test

Development tool, not part of the app. Japanese Pokémon cards from many sets, each made into a fake phone photo from its picture (TCGdex's, or Scrydex's up to the set's printed size: see japanesePictures in japanese.js), run through the app's Japanese reader and search (japanese-reader.js, japanese.js), and scored. The real Japanese photos, when there are some, are in the private dev-local/ folder, listed with the right answers in dev-local/japanese-photos.json. It asks TCGdex and Scrydex a few questions each.
