Kortpris learning test

Development tool, not part of the app. It checks learned.js with the real photos in dev-local/ (never published, listed in dev-local/real-photos.json). First every photo is learned, as if the viewer had picked its card. Then a made-up second photo of each (tilted, moved, other light, blurred, the same every run) must be recognised as its own card. And each photo must not be recognised as anything while its own card isn't learned: that is the check against opening the wrong card.
