Kortpris learning test

Development tool, not part of the app. It checks learned.js with the real photos in dev-local/ (never published, listed in dev-local/real-photos.json). First every photo is learned, as if the viewer had picked its card. Then a made-up second photo of each (tilted, moved, other light, blurred, the same every run) must be recognised as its own card. And each photo must not be recognised as anything while its own card isn't learned: that is the check against opening the wrong card. A harder second photo of each (other light, a glare, more tilt; every other one also small and soft, so its print can't be read) may not be recognised for sure, but its card may still be suggested: then the app's search is run, and should show it first. The app must never open a wrong card, with or without suggestions. It also counts the second photos the app opens before reading any text (when the card's own edges are found, see openLearnedEarly in app.js), and times finding the card and comparing it.
