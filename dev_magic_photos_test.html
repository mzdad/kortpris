Kortpris Magic photo test

Development tool, not part of the app. Runs real phone photos of Magic: The Gathering cards through the app's Magic reader and search (magic-reader.js). The photos live in dev-local/, which is never published, listed with the right answers in dev-local/magic-photos.json. It asks Scryfall, one question at a time.
