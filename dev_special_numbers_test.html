Kortpris special numbers test

Development tool, not part of the app. Cards whose number isn't "number/set size": Black Star promos ("SWSH050", "SVP EN 001"), Galarian and Trainer Gallery ("GG01/GG70", "TG05/TG30"), Shiny Vault ("SV1/SV94"), Radiant Collection ("RC1/RC32") and Aquapolis/Skyridge holos ("H1/H32"). It makes a fake phone photo of each from the database's own picture, runs it through the app's reader and search, and scores the result. The last column shows how the number corners were read.
