Kortpris full-art test

Development tool, not part of the app. Full-art, gold, rainbow and illustration-rare cards, whose name and number are printed on the artwork, from 2012 to 2025. It makes a fake phone photo of each from the database's own picture, runs it through the app's reader and search, and scores the result. The last column says what the name strip (see readNameStrip in reader.js) read.
